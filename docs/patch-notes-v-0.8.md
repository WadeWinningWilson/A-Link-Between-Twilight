# Patch Notes for v-0.8 (alpha)

**A Link Between Twilight** — pre-release alpha. Changes since [v0.55](patch-notes-v0.55.md).

This is an **alpha**: expect unfinished fights, optional systems that are still being tested, and the occasional crash or odd save if you turn everything on at once. Toggle features under **Settings → ALBW**.

Full setting and gameplay list: **[ALBT-Features-Overview.md](ALBT-Features-Overview.md)**.

---

## New since v0.55

### Settings → ALBW

Mod options now live on their own pause **ALBW** tab: **Systems**, **Difficulty**, **Quality of Life**, **Master Quest**, and **Visuals**.

### Extra Item Slot + Quick Equip Wheel (optional)

- **Extra + Quick Swap:** Up cycles swords, Right cycles shields, **Down quickswaps outfits**. Map defaults to M / Tab (rebind Open Map if you have no touchpad — L3 recommended).
- **Quick Equip Wheel:** hold the item-wheel bind to assign a tool to **Z** only.

### Soul of Light (optional)

Formerly Death Recovery Orb. After Talo is rescued, dying can halve your wallet and leave a **Soul of Light** at the death spot to recover part of what was lost. Item strip and meter refill on death are unchanged.

### Wolf Link Combat (optional)

Bites build charges for **Midna Charge Attacks**, plus new **wolf howl** and **Midna Arm** attacks. Charge attacks deal more damage to twilight enemies and freeze non-twilight enemies. Wolf Howl plays new songs as you collect them.

### Boss Refinement (optional)

New moves, phases, and sword use on fights that normally require a specific weapon.

- **Diababa, Fyrus, Armogohma** — nearly finalized, not polished.
- **Morpheel** — early stages.
- **Stallord, Blizzeta, Argorok, Zant, Ganon / Ganondorf** — still vanilla.

The Armogohma reveal model needs the **Armogohma Custom** companion pack **and** this setting on.

### Master Quest (optional)

Recommended once per playthrough. Halves heart-container and heart-piece rewards. The Postman sells heart, stamina / Magic Meter, and sword upgrades. Dungeon meter growth is disabled in favor of those shop purchases.

### Also in this alpha

- **ALBW Magic Meter** for human Link (restored Magic Meter as an ALBW-style stamina bar).
- Shield **Parry & Bash**, **Manual Shielding**, **Shield Durability**, and **Parry Master**.
- **Focused Arts** (hidden-skill charge bank and finishers).
- **Outfit Stats** and Sumo fists-only visual.
- Enemy **HP multipliers**, **Region Damage / Region Multipliers**, **Enemy Death Rupees**.
- Cycle Z-targeting (**Stick Cycle Lock-on**).
- Companion-mod installer (`companion_mods\_release\`) for Armogohma Custom, MM Skull Kid, and Wind Waker skins.

## Known issues (alpha — still ironing out)

These are the rough edges worth calling out for this build. Trim or add from play.

**Likely crash / save risk if turned on** (the pause menu already warns these are not fully tested):

- **Outfit Stats**
- **Boss Refinement** (especially unfinished fights and the Armogohma reveal pack)
- **Shade's Refuge** and **Realtime Potions**
- **Deku Leaf Glide**

**Unfinished or missing polish**

- Boss Refinement coverage is incomplete (see above). Diababa / Fyrus / Armogohma still need a polish pass; Morpheel is early.
- Master Quest stamina upgrade row still uses the wrong shop icon.
- Enemy HP sliders are not true max-HP scaling yet — very high multipliers can feel “stuck” on weak hits.
- Final shop / wallet pricing pass is not done.
- Shade's Refuge, Realtime Potions, and Deku Leaf Glide are WIP even when the toggle is on.

**Suggested play for a first alpha run**

Leave the untested Systems off unless you are specifically trying them. Master Quest is meant as a once-per-playthrough choice. Turn **Boss Refinement** on only if you want the in-progress dungeon fights (and install the Armogohma pack for that reveal).
